1. Executive Summary
KOITO & CHAIK is a luxury invitation platform that transforms a traditional wedding invitation into a private digital experience.
Rather than simply directing guests to a public wedding website, every printed invitation becomes a personalized digital key.
Each guest receives:
* a beautifully designed printed invitation
* a unique QR code
* a unique Secret PIN
The QR code opens the website.
The Secret PIN unlocks that guest's private experience.
This creates exclusivity while eliminating the need for complicated scanning systems, expensive hardware, or enterprise-level backend infrastructure.
The website should feel less like a website and more like entering a private members' club.

36. Guiding Product Principles
Every design and engineering decision should be measured against these principles:
1. Luxury over complexity — remove anything that doesn't elevate the experience.
2. Privacy by design — guests should feel their invitation is personal and exclusive.
3. Simplicity wins — no unnecessary logins, downloads, or hardware.
4. Emotion before information — tell a story before presenting logistics.
5. Mobile-first excellence — most guests will experience the site on their phones.
6. Timeless aesthetics — avoid trends that will quickly feel dated.
7. Craft every interaction — each animation, transition, and detail should reinforce the feeling of entering a private celebration.

Product Vision Statement
KOITO & CHAIK is more than a wedding website—it is a private digital extension of the wedding invitation itself. Every invitation is a key, every guest is personally welcomed, and every interaction is crafted to evoke the elegance, intimacy, and exclusivity of a once-in-a-lifetime celebration.
